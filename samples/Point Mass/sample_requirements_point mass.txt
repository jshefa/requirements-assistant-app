10. The System shall operate in an atmosphere comparable to that of Earth. Assume 21 degrees centigrade [70 degrees Fahrenheit], with low humidity, and pressurized to 100 kPA [750 mm Hg, 14.5 psi].
11. The System shall not contribute any particulates (e.g. dust) to the ISS atmosphere.
12. The System shall enclose all lubricated components to prevent lubricants from leaking into the atmosphere of the ISS.
13. The System shall not use more than 1.5 Watt-hours to support all operations. Note: Assume a maximum of 1 minute of operations.
